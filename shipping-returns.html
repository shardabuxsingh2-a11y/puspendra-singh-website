Shipping & Returns

Information about delivery, damaged items, cancellations and return enquiries.

Last updated: 9 October 2026

1. Shipping coverage

Available delivery locations and shipping options are confirmed individually when an order is discussed. Please share the correct delivery address and phone number when confirming an order.

2. Processing and delivery estimates

Any processing time, courier details, shipping fee and estimated delivery date will be communicated before the order is finalised. Delivery estimates are not guaranteed and may be affected by courier delays, weather or circumstances outside our control.

3. Order tracking and support

For order updates, contact us using the phone number or WhatsApp button on this website. Please provide your order details so we can identify your enquiry.

4. Damaged, incorrect or missing items

If an item arrives damaged, incorrect or incomplete, contact us as soon as reasonably possible after delivery. Include your order reference and clear photographs where relevant so we can review the issue and explain the available resolution.

5. Returns and exchanges

Return or exchange eligibility depends on the product and the terms confirmed before purchase. Contact us before sending an item back; unapproved returns may not be identifiable or accepted. Items should generally be unused and returned with original tags or packaging where applicable, unless the item is damaged or incorrect or applicable law provides otherwise.

6. Cancellations

Contact us promptly if you need to cancel an order. Whether cancellation is possible may depend on whether the order has already been processed or dispatched. Any applicable refund or cancellation terms will be confirmed with you.

7. Refunds

If a refund is approved, we will confirm the amount, method and expected processing timeline directly. Processing time may depend on the payment provider or bank. No specific refund period is promised by this general policy.
