Privacy Policy

How we collect and use information when you contact us or place an apparel order.

Last updated: 9 October 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Pushpendra Singh (“we”, “us” or “our”) handles personal information relating to customer enquiries and apparel or clothing-accessory orders.

2. Information we collect

Depending on your interaction with us, we may collect:

  • Your name;
  • Phone number;
  • Email address, if you provide it;
  • Order and delivery details you choose to provide, such as products requested and delivery address; and
  • Messages you send about products, orders, shipping, returns or support.

For apparel orders and related customer enquiries, we specifically collect your name and phone number so we can communicate with you about the enquiry or order.

3. How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, confirm product availability and order details, coordinate delivery, provide customer support, handle returns or complaints, and maintain records necessary for business operations.

4. WhatsApp and other service providers

If you choose to contact us through WhatsApp, your conversation is also subject to WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy. We do not control the privacy practices of third-party services.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell customer personal information. We may share the minimum information needed with service providers involved in completing an order, such as delivery or payment providers, or where disclosure is required by law.

6. Retention and security

We keep information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, including customer support, business records and legal obligations. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable recordkeeping or legal requirements. You can also choose not to provide optional information, although this may limit our ability to fulfil an order or enquiry.

8. Children

Our website is intended for general customers and is not directed specifically to children. Please do not send children’s personal information unless you are authorised to do so.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
